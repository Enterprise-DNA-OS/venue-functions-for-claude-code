# The venue working desk

The base serves an owner or function coordinator who wants bookings, deposits and kitchen handovers in a database they own. Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode or Cursor reads the same instructions and runs the same tested commands. Read-only HTML supplies the diary, money desk and kitchen sheets without a server.

A calendar screen gives drag and drop, a phone app gives mobile capture, and an offline app keeps working without a connection. This base does not provide those experiences. It also has no customer booking portal, online payments, live email intake or shared scheduling lock. Keep existing capture processes until the replacement is tested. Enterprise DNA can build the screens and connections the venue needs as part of a scoped version.

The software reports conflicts rather than preventing every conflicting booking. Operators review tentative holds and overlapping staff before confirming arrangements. If the venue needs continuous interactive scheduling for its whole team, scope that work before switching. Nothing here sends a message or processes money.
